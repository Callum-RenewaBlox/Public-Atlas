Privacy notice

How we use your information

RenewaBlox helps businesses cut the cost of their electricity, working with the supplier tem. This notice explains what we hold about you, where it came from, why we use it and what you can ask us to do.

Last updated 12 October 2026

Who we are

RenewaBlox Ltd is the data controller. We are registered in England & Wales, company number 13793455, at Priestfield Farm, Henfield Road, Albourne, Hassocks BN6 9DE.

For anything about your information, email callum@renewablox.com.

If we emailed you first

We contact businesses whose electricity use suggests they could save with tem. To do that we hold:

  • Business details: the business name, its sites and sector, and our own estimate of how much electricity it uses.
  • Work contact details of the person we write to: name, job title, work email address and work phone number.

Where it came from: public sources only, such as the business’s own website, its published accounts and public registers like Companies House and sector regulators’ registers. We do not buy contact lists.

Why we may use it: our legitimate interest in telling businesses about a service that could lower their energy costs. We only write to business addresses, every email says how to opt out, and if you tell us to stop we will not contact you again.

If you send us a bill or use the bill check

When you use the form on this site or email us, we receive your email address and what you choose to send: your MPAN (supply number), your contract end date, and any bill. A bill can also show names, addresses, account numbers, consumption and charges.

We use it to work out what you could save and, if you want a quote, to request one from tem. We only pass your supply details to tem once you have agreed to it, usually by signing a letter of authority. The basis is our legitimate interest in answering your request, or the steps you ask us to take before a contract.

Who we share it with

  • tem (tem.energy), the electricity supplier we work with, only when you have asked for a quote or contract. If you sign with tem, tem pays RenewaBlox a commission, which is shown in your contract.
  • The service providers we use to run our business: email, website hosting and form handling, our customer records database and office software. They act on our instructions and may not use your information for anything else.

Some of these providers store data outside the UK, including in the United States. Where they do, we rely on the safeguards UK law provides, such as the UK–US data bridge or contract clauses approved for UK transfers.

We never sell your information.

How we prepare emails

We use software, including AI tools, to research businesses and draft emails. A person at RenewaBlox reviews and approves our emails before they are sent. We do not make any decision about you by automated means alone.

How long we keep it

  • Outreach contacts who don’t reply: deleted within 12 months of our last email.
  • If you opt out: we keep only your email address and business name on a do-not-contact list, so we don’t write to you again.
  • Bill checks and quotes that go no further: deleted within 12 months.
  • If you sign a contract: for the length of the contract and six years after it ends, for our accounts and any later questions about it.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your information, to correct it, to delete it, to limit how we use it, or to move it to someone else. You can object to us using it, and you can tell us at any time to stop contacting you; we will. Email callum@renewablox.com and we will reply within a month.

If you’re unhappy with how we have used your information, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.
