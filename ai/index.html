Inside OSCAR

Three cards of inputs

Fill them in, then choose who funds the CAPEX and how the revenue is shared over the contract, five years by default. Everything is in GBP.

Miner & Market Data

Eleven miners from MicroBT and Bitmain, air and hydro, each with its rated hash rate and power draw. Pick one and its hash rate and power draw fill in, to adjust if you need to; the unit cost is yours to enter. The Whatsminers and the Antminer S21 XP Hydro have overclock profiles.

Site Inputs

Available power, capacity factor, what the power earns today and the cost of power. Bitcoin mining is always on; energy markets (export, demand turn-up and the capacity market), heat off-take and a Phase 2 expansion can be added. Or load a project you saved earlier.

Modelling assumptions

The Bitcoin price in GBP and the 30-day average network hash rate, fetched live from CoinGecko and mempool.space (or Blockchain.com). Then the pool fee, the CAPEX for racking, heat exchangers, dry coolers and labour, a contingency, and a market model for how the Bitcoin price and the hash rate grow each year:

Growth each year by market model
BaseBTC +35%Hash +7.1%
BearBTC +20%Hash +11.3%
BullBTC +50%Hash +3%

OSCAR’s figures are indicative, depend on the inputs and market model chosen, and are not investment advice.
