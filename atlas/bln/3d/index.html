What is measured, and what is assumed

So the engineering review knows what to trust. The building and machine come from the drawing and the site photographs; where neither says, the model says so here.

From the drawing and the site photographs

Substructure drawing C-301 rev A (2016), the October 2026 site photographs, the Design Overview of 16 October 2026 and the client's notes of 21 October 2026

  • Portal frame: 3 frames at 4.264 m centres, 5.486 m span; slab 9.0 × 6.1 m
  • Levels: floor +184.000, machine area +183.700, grid compound +183.200
  • Door and apron: a 1.75 m opening on the north wall, a 3.55 × 1.60 m apron, a 600 mm manhole
  • Machine: the gate valve, bifurcation, generator, spear valves and casing at their positions along the axis
  • Inlet chamber: 5.6 × 2.85 m, wall top +185.323
  • Inside: white liner, monorail crane, louvres and trenches; the Gilkes panels 950 mm off the east gable with a clear walkway from the turbine casing; the BLOX cabinet in line with them in the gap beside the HMI panel, with 0.75 m of hot-air space behind it
  • BLOX cabinet: 800 × 1000 × 2200 mm, holding 20 miners, three to a shelf and two on the top shelf, with the network switch and edge controller on edge beside the shelves (photograph of 21 October)
  • Ventilation: two 450 mm extraction fans in the gable behind the cabinet, one above the timber batten and one below; a thermostat runs one, then both as the space warms
  • Technical dispatch rule: the miners follow the export. One starts while export sits at the 250 kW limit, another each time it stays there; at or under 245 kW they stop one at a time

Assumed: please check

Each is one setting in the model, so a measurement replaces it directly

  • Room length: the east bay is drawn 0.85 m longer than the 2016 drawing (grid 3 at 9.38 m, not 8.53 m) so that the walkway seen on site and the service space behind the panels and the cabinet both fit. The engineers confirm both exist; two tape measurements (casing end to panel front, panel back to wall) will replace the stretch
  • Exterior colours: green vertical-rib cladding and a grey roof; no exterior photograph was available
  • Heights: eaves 3.95 m, a 15° pitch, ridge 4.77 m, scaled from photographs
  • South-wall louvre, and the exact bracing and trench layout
  • Terrain, burn, track, fence and pole line: procedural and indicative, not surveyed
  • Fan heights: 2.55 m and 1.50 m to the fans' centres, read from the sketch, both on the cabinet's centre line; about 4,900 m³/h each is a typical figure for a 450 mm wall fan, not a chosen model
  • Heat enclosure (option): 18 mm OSB, as wide as the cabinet, from its rear panel to the gable and up to 2.86 m, just under the gable rail
  • Technical dispatch simulation: the river's power through the day is invented to show the rule at work; each miner draws 3.4 kW at the wall, so 15 run at the full surplus; the thermostat is illustrative: the lead fan comes on at 24 °C as the first miners warm the space and runs while any miner does, and the second comes in at 32 °C and goes off at 21 °C, each with a minimum run time so they do not chatter
  • Orientation: the model is drawn with its axis east–west and the door to the north, lit from the south-east. The aerial imagery (May 2025) shows the building's axis running south-west to north-east, about 43°, with the grid compound off the north-east gable
