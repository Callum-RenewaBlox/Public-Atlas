Assumptions

What the four days are built from, so the engineering and commercial reviews know what to trust.

Measured
  • Market price: Elexon Market Index Data (APX), half-hourly, for each of the four days. A short-term market index, not a day-ahead auction price
  • GB wind (onshore and offshore) and solar generation: Elexon's actual generation by type, half-hourly
Set for this model
  • The strike, £87.51/MWh, is the value of mining per MWh used across RenewaBlox's November 2026 models
  • The CHP's whole net output goes one way each half hour: to the grid when the market price beats the strike, otherwise to the BLOX. 444 kW, 0.222 MWh a half hour
  • BLOX revenue is valued at the strike; grid revenue at the half hour's market price
Assumed
  • The plant runs at a steady 444 kW net through each of the four days: the consented 499 kW less about 11 % for its own load (assumed; metered data would replace it)
  • Each half hour is decided with hindsight of its own price
  • The cable routes and the lights in the plates, which are rendered from the concept model
  • The transformer and the grid route are indicative
Not in the four days
  • Export is valued at the market price alone. Embedded benefits such as gDUoS credits and triad payments, and route-to-market uplifts, are left out

Concept model, November 2026. Market price and generation by type: Elexon BMRS. Contains BMRS data © Elexon Limited copyright and database right 2026. Sunrise and sunset for Ross-on-Wye. Each half hour is decided with hindsight of its own price: an operating record would differ.
