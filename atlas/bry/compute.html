What is measured, and what is assumed

So the engineering review knows what to trust. The container, the cooler and the breakers come from documents; the pad, the wall, the tanks and the compound are scaled from the site photographs and the satellite imagery; the routes and the terrain are assumptions, and the model says so here.

From documents and the site photographs

The Green Hash 410 kW outdoor hydro proposal, the 27 site photographs of 13 October 2026, and the client’s notes of 24 and 25 October

  • Container: a 10 ft high-cube, 2.991 × 2.438 × 2.896 m external (the Green Hash specification)
  • Dry cooler: 3.64 × 2.306 × 2.50 m, six fans, V-coil (the specification); its layout drawing gives the 2.85 m gap to the container
  • Rack: one rack of 3 × 20 slots, 172.8 kW rated and 200 kW at most including cooling (the client’s figure). The render fits 30 miners, 5.76 kW each at the rating, where the revenue-share model counts 24 at 7.2 kW: the same 172.8 kW, a count to confirm. The Atlas notes record that the site may grow to 400 kW
  • Supply: the spare 400 A outgoing way in the LV panel, an ABB SACE Emax 2 air circuit breaker (the panel carries 400 A and 630 A frames in the photographs). The panel stays as it is: only a new outgoing cable is added
  • 11 kV ring main unit: a Schneider Ringmaster CE2 on legs on a plinth, with a canopy (photographed)
  • Transformer 3: Wilson Power Solutions, grey and finned, about 2.5 × 1.6 × 2.2 m (scaled from the photographs)
  • Placement: the cooler on the ground beside the container, not on its roof (24 October), and the container on the east side of the pad next to the power compound for the shortest cable run (25 October), with no vehicles on the pad

Scaled from the photographs and the satellite (±15 %)

Google Maps satellite at zoom 21 (0.052 m per pixel; it predates the photographs), the Google Earth oblique, and the photographs

  • Pad: about 12 × 10 m free once the trailer and the telehandler are moved
  • Retaining wall: about 2.0 m high and 0.3 m thick, with the upper hardstanding behind it at +2.0 m carrying the Trioliet feeder, the feed tank on the Atlas map (about 8.4 × 2.6 m, 2.4 m high above the wall top)
  • Pipe gantry along the wall top: two silver pipes of about DN150 at 2.3 to 2.7 m, and a red heating pipe
  • Digester: about 26 m across, a 6 m wall and a dome 5 m above it, its centre about 20 m behind the wall; the small ring tank about 10 m across and 5 m high, west of the pad
  • CHP: two lime-green containers of about 12 × 3 × 3 m with black radiator banks on top, 14 to 20 m east of the pad; the stack about 16 m with its platform at 10 m
  • Purple containers: two, each about 3.0 × 2.2 × 2.3 m, south-east of the pad
  • Heras fence: 3.5 m panels 2 m high between the pad and the transformer plinth, with a grated trench about 1 m wide along it
  • Sunken pit: about 3 × 2 m at the pad’s south-west corner (from the Atlas)

Assumed: please check

Each is one setting in the model, so a measurement or a decision replaces it directly

  • Layout on the pad: the container’s east face 1.8 m from the Heras line and its doors facing the open pad, 2.8 m clear of the wall; the cooler west of it at the 2.85 m gap, on two 4.69 × 0.4 m concrete strips with its long axis parallel to the container, 2.5 m from the wall and 2.5 m from the pit, nothing else within 6 m of it
  • Flow and return: DN100 insulated (red to the cooler, blue back) out of the container’s west wall, where the pump cabinet with the glazed door is, down to 0.55 m on galvanised stands across the gap into the cooler’s headers, with isolation valves and a yellow step-over
  • Cable route: a 4-core 240 mm² XLPE/SWA cable (about 400 A) from the spare breaker, in a 0.4 m trench across the fence line to a 400 A isolator on the container’s east side, about 5 m in all (LV panel, plinth edge, a draw pit at the fence, the riser); shown as a reinstated strip in the plinth and the pad, with a cable-protector ramp where it crosses the trench grating
  • Container colour: RenewaBlox light grey (RAL 7035) with a RenewaBlox blue band; the Atlas shows it white
  • Terrain beyond the pad: wooded banks to the north, east and south and the chip yard to the west are indicative only (no LiDAR; the Google Earth mesh is too coarse here)
  • Vehicles: none on the pad (the trailer and the telehandler moved off it, as agreed); the white trailer and a van stand off the pad to the south-west by the sheds, where the imagery had them, and are indicative only
