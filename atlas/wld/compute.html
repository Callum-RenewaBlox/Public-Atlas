What is measured, and what is assumed

So the engineering review knows what to trust. The site comes from government open data: the ground, the heights and the tank sizes from the Environment Agency’s LiDAR, the buildings and roads from Ordnance Survey, the fields from the Crop Map of England. The BLOX kit comes from documents. Where the BLOX goes on the site, and how it connects, are assumptions, and the model says so here.

Measured from open data

Environment Agency LiDAR (survey of 10–22 November 2020, 1 m), OS OpenMap Local and the RPA Crop Map of England 2024

  • Ground: the 1 m LiDAR terrain; the pad ground is 2.5 m above sea level (ODN) and varies by −0.09 to +0.25 m across the pad, so the model levels it under the slab
  • Storage tank: about 29 m across, its dome top 12.8 m (planning gave 32 m and 13 m; the model follows the LiDAR)
  • Digester: about 21 m across, its top 12.1 m; the two hydrolysis tanks about 9 m across, flat tops at 5.7–6.4 m
  • CHP enclosure: 11.5 × 5.6 m on a bearing of 128°, its roof plant up to 6.3 m
  • Silage clamps: a 54 × 50 m walled block, heaps about 3.2 m high; the pump room 12 × 11 m between the tanks
  • Containment bund: an earth bank about 0.8 m high round the tank compound
  • Poultry sheds: eight, 92.7 × 21 m, eaves 3.45 m, ridge 6.0 m, their centres 270–370 m north-east
  • Trees and hedges: 1,017 crowns and the hedgerows from the vegetation model; the tree belt starts 4.9 m off the pad’s south-west edge

From documents

The Winchain quotation GH000213L and RenewaBlox’s model of the same kit, and the planning report for the plant

  • Container: a Winchain 20 ft high-cube hydro-cooling container, 6.058 × 2.438 × 2.896 m external
  • Racks: three, 800 × 700 × 2,000 mm, 20 slots each, with a smart PDU, a switch and a manifold pair each
  • Dry cooler: a Winchain ExaDC410, 3.64 × 2.306 × 2.50 m, six fans, on a 0.45 m steel frame on the container roof; DN80 flow and return
  • Services: the pump line and CDU panel at the services end, fire and leak detection, CCTV and lighting
  • Silage clamps: three bays with walls up to 4.5 m (planning)

Assumed: please check

Each is one setting in the model, so a measurement or a decision replaces it directly

  • Position: the pad on the grass verge north-west of the CHP, its long axis on 135°/315°, chosen by eye and not measured
  • Pad: 10 × 7 m concrete, 0.15 m thick, with a 2 m mesh fence and a double gate on the side facing the CHP
  • Interior layout: the racks along the north-east wall facing a 1.2 m aisle, the services end towards the CHP, free floor at the north-west end for more racks; the pump, vessels and panel positions
  • Cable: from the LV pillar through a draw pit, out of the pad and about 10 m in all in a trench to a cable entry on the CHP enclosure; the route and the entry point are indicative
  • Branding: the RenewaBlox livery, the wordmark and the “BLOX.” marks, including the fascia round the cooler’s fan deck
  • Plant details: the CHP enclosure’s louvres, doors and roof kit, the flare (not seen in the data), the colours, the October look of each field (from its Crop Map crop), the solar roof’s shed, and the widths of the roads and tracks
