Assumptions

What the page is built from, so the engineering and commercial reviews know what to trust.

Measured
  • Wildmore's metered export, all 17,520 half hours of 2025
  • Market price: Elexon Market Index Data, half-hourly. A short-term market index, not a day-ahead auction price
Published
  • gDUoS: NGED East Midlands, HV generation, 2024/25 and 2025/26, by red, amber and green band
  • EET: NESO's East Midlands tariff, on the three Triad half hours inside 2025
Set for this model
  • Strike: 8p/kWh, fixed all year. It values every kWh delivered to the BLOX, cooling included, and is not 2025's actual mining revenue
  • The whole output switches each half hour
  • BLOX capacity: 444 kW, servers and cooling; export above that goes to the grid
Assumed
  • The HV tariff applies to this connection
  • The 11 half hours with no market trades are filled between their neighbours
  • Energy partners uplift of 15% and P442 uplift of £25/MWh: working figures, to be confirmed at tender
  • The transformer's position, the grid cable's route, and the lights in the plates
  • Each half hour is decided with hindsight of its own price

Not modelled: supply contract terms, BSUoS, line losses, supplier fees, DUoS fixed and reactive charges, REGOs and the Capacity Market.
