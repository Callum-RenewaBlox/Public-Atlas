Assumptions

What the four days are built from, so the engineering and commercial reviews know what to trust.

Measured
  • Market price: Elexon Market Index Data (APX), half-hourly, for each of the four days. A short-term market index, not a day-ahead auction price
  • GB wind (onshore and offshore) and solar generation: Elexon's actual generation by type, half-hourly
Set for this model
  • Strike: £87.51/MWh, fixed for every half hour. It is the BLOX's five-year average mining value per MWh in the Finance tab's base case, on OSCAR's Bitcoin inputs
  • The plant's whole 28 MW net output goes one way each half hour: to the grid when the market price beats the strike, otherwise to the BLOX. 14 MWh a half hour
  • BLOX revenue is valued at the strike; grid revenue at the half hour's market price
Assumed
  • The plant runs at its full 28 MW net through each of the four days
  • Each half hour is decided with hindsight of its own price
  • The cable routes and the lights in the plates, which are rendered from the concept model
Not in the four days
  • tem's uplift on the export price, REGOs, gDUoS, P442 and the Capacity Market: all of these are in the Finance tab
  • The Finance tab sends half the running hours each way, as OSCAR's model does; these days show which half hours the BLOX would take

Concept model, November 2026. Market price and generation by type: Elexon BMRS. Contains BMRS data © Elexon Limited copyright and database right 2026. Sunrise and sunset for Inverurie. Each half hour is decided with hindsight of its own price: an operating record would differ.
