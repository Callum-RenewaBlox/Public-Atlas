01AD · Biogas · CHP
Fuelled Renewables
Your engine runs steady. The price doesn’t.
Anaerobic digestion, biogas and CHP: generation you fuel and run all year, sold into a power price that changes every half hour — and on some half hours falls below zero. Compute on site gives the cheap half hours somewhere better to go.
Interactive 3D Live dispatch · every half hour
The model
Peaker Plant model
A 499 kWe biogas CHP with a BLOX compute load bank beside it. Its 350 kW tradable block follows the price, half-hour by half-hour.
  • Above the strike price, the block exports to the grid.
  • Below it, the compute load bank absorbs it on site instead.
  • Drive the dispatch desk through four real GB price days.

02Hydro · Off-grid · Constrained
Stranded Renewables
Power with nowhere to go, used where it’s made.
Generation the grid can’t take, or won’t pay enough for — no export connection, a constrained one, or an asset that no longer pays its way. Compute co-located with the plant turns that output into revenue, right where it is generated.
Interactive 3D Island mode · load-following
The model
Run-of-River Hydro model
A 999 kW Gilkes Turgo in the Scottish Highlands, once a stranded and distressed asset, with a BLOX data centre in the powerhouse running in island mode.
  • The controller tracks the river; the compute fleet follows the power available.
  • Hydro-cooled: the racks’ heat returns to the river — no fans, no compressors.
  • Run a rainy week hour by hour, or a full hydrological year.

How we work with generators
Compute, at the point of generation.
Whatever you generate, the pattern is the same: a working model first, then the compute on site, then every half hour decided for you.
