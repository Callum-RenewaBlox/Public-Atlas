The foundation · in every tier
RED and RED Plus, from tem.
Whichever tier you choose, your supply comes from tem: power priced against UK renewable generators, with the RED Plus benefit on top. It’s the first saving on the bill, before RenewaBlox changes anything on site.
RED
Power priced against UK renewablestem’s intelligent Rosso platform
Most suppliers buy your energy on the wholesale market and pass on its costs and markups. tem don’t. Instead they build a bespoke price for your business, balanced against their own portfolio of UK renewable generators — so you pay closer to the true cost of energy.
  • Your unit rate is fixed for the length of your contract
  • Industry non-power costs pass through at cost, with nothing added
  • An itemised quote that maps line by line onto your invoice
RED+
A renewable generator linked to your usageP442 · exempt supply
Under P442, eligible UK renewable generation — such as solar or wind — is linked to your business’s usage. On the share it covers, certain non-commodity costs, the government scheme levies, aren’t charged. That saving is your RED Plus benefit, on top of tem’s price.
  • Shown as a p/kWh benefit in your quote, and credited monthly
  • Its own line on your invoice: ‘RED Plus benefit’
  • In every new tem contract from day one — no extra paperwork
  • Your supply is unchanged: power still arrives through the grid
