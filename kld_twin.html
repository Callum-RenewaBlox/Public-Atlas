What is measured, and what is assumed

So the engineering review knows what to trust. The site is a Gaussian-splat capture made from drone video and photographs on 8 June 2026, outside the powerhouse and inside it. The data centre is drawn where its layout was taped out on the hall floor, read from the site photographs in 3D. What the tape does not fix is assumed, and the model says so here.

Measured

The captures of 8 June 2026 (drone video and GPS-tagged photographs outside, drone video inside) and the photographs of the taped layout

  • The site: georeferenced from the photographs’ GPS, to about 0.6 m, its heading checked to 2° against the aircraft; the interior capture is aligned to it
  • Hall: 11.6 × 9.0 m inside (12.2 × 9.6 m outside); the length from the capture, ±0.1 m, the depth from the 2018 drawings, as the rear wall is buried in the bank
  • Main door: 3.5 m wide, from the capture (the jambs measure 3.53 m inside)
  • Layout: the rack bays, the pump skid, the heat-exchanger plinth and the pipe routes from the tape, read in 3D off the site photographs through their registered cameras; every edge checked is within 5 cm of the tape
  • Racks: four 0.80 m bays in one row, 3.2 m along the east-north-east end wall, backs about 0.6 m off it, rack 4 by the main-door wall
  • Pump skid and plinth: the skid 0.99 × 0.355 m and the heat-exchanger plinth 0.68 × 1.01 m, as taped
  • Penstock: the Y-piece and the items round it from the interior capture and the photographs, about ±3–5 cm (±6 cm at the tee); none of it is surveyed

From documents

The detailed design for the cooling island, the rack supplier’s proposal, the equipment datasheets and the 2018 drawings of the powerhouse

  • Racks: hydro-cooled DCX racks on a 0.8 × 1.2 m footprint, 26 M63S++ miners each, 104 in all: 748 kW, and up to about 1 MW
  • River-water spur: a DN150 tee off the penstock downstream of the main inlet valve, then a gate valve, a duplex strainer and a DN100 control valve at their datasheet lengths, and stainless steel after the control valve
  • Hall depth and walls: 9.0 m inside, with 300 mm walls (the drawings)

Assumed: please check

Each is one setting in the model, so a measurement or a decision replaces it directly

  • Rack height: 2.18 m as quoted, a placeholder under confirmation with the supplier. It is known to be short: 26 miners at 2U take 2.31 m before the frame and PDUs, so the model draws them closer together than they can stand
  • Heat exchanger: one plate unit, as taped; its type, its 0.62 m frame width and its nozzle heights are not measured
  • Pump: one in-line pump on the skid, as taped; only one fits the skid’s 0.355 m width
  • Heights: every height above the floor, the tape being flat: the pipe runs (supply 0.62 m, return 0.30 m), the headers over the racks (2.32 m) and the canopy
  • Inside the skid and beyond the tape: the pump, vessel and control box, the secondary outflow’s route, the cable ladder’s route and the canopy’s extent
  • Finishes: the kit’s colours are indicative, as in the renders
